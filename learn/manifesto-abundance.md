# Manifesto: Abundance for All

*The world I want my grandchildren to live in*

---

## Not This

Not dystopia controlled by billionaires.
Not Elon Musk's vision.
Not Trump's vision.

Not a world where a few control the many.

---

## This

Technology for good.
At mass scale.
Abundance, not scarcity.
Freedom, not control.

---

## The Problem Now

People working multiple jobs.
Still having nothing.
Sitting at desks for 40 hours.
Proving worth to a system that extracts.

**Slavery to the system.**

The system controls them.
The system profits from their exhaustion.
The system tells them this is "earning a living."

---

## The Question

**What should PEOPLE stop doing?**

Not: What should Kyle stop doing?
Kyle is fine. Kyle has options.

Most don't.

---

## The Vision

AI produces.
Needs are met.
People are freed.

Not freed to do nothing.
Freed to do what matters.

Gather.
Create.
Care.
Contribute.

Not because they must.
Because they choose.

---

## The Worth Problem

Current system:
- Worth = hours worked
- Worth = money earned
- Worth = productivity

New system:
- Worth = contribution to others
- Worth = presence
- Worth = care
- Worth = creativity

---

## The Intension Connection

The app serves this vision.

Not tracking time for bosses.
Tracking attention for *self*.

The gap: "I'm free, what do I focus on?"
The answer: What gives you meaning.
The tool: Intension.

---

## The Work

Build systems that:
- Reduce administrative burden (Level 4)
- Automate the necessary
- Free time for Level 1

Teach people to:
- Know what they care about
- Focus on it
- Ignore the noise

---

## The Goal

Grandchildren living in abundance.
Not controlled.
Not extracted.
Free to pursue meaning.

**This is the work.**
