## Definition

**Intension** (philosophy/logic): The internal content of a concept—what it *means*, its defining properties.

**Extension**: The external referents—what the concept *points to*, the things it applies to.

**Example:**
- "Prime number" — intension: divisible only by 1 and itself
- "Prime number" — extension: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11...
