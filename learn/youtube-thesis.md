Stories, frameworks, and lessons from 18 years of building data systems at scale.

## The Launch Strategy (As Of June 2026)

### Mistakes to avoid

1. **Don't optimize for the algorithm before establishing the voice.** The Daniel Pink / Doctor Alex pattern works because viewers can feel the credentialed depth. Faking algorithm patterns destroys that.
2. **Don't grind for shorts.** Per the cinematic-video doctrine, medium-form is the destination. Shorts are derivative compression for distribution, not the work itself.
3. **Don't lead with "AI." Lead with the thing AI helps you do better.** The article's exploding channels lead with *outcomes* (writing, medicine, money) — not the tool.
4. **Don't try to be the workshop seller on YouTube.** Per the North Star, services are Tier 3. The channel is Tier 1 creation. If the workshop gets sold, it's because the channel earned the trust — not because the channel is a funnel.

### What to actually do

1. **Pick a banner format.** Recommendation: 8-minute medium-form, talking head + B-roll, single-idea-per-video, no intro music, no "hey what's up YouTube." Just the substance. Begin in the middle.
2. **Pick 10 single-idea topics** across the three pillars and outline them all before producing one. (Quality of the queue determines the quality of the channel.)
3. **Produce one per week.** Not three. Not daily. One excellent medium-form video per week is the entire game.
4. **Re-cut into shorts only after the long-form lands.** Shorts are the distribution residue, not the source.
5. **Track the right metric.** Not subs. Not views. **Average view duration as % of length, and watch-to-comment ratio.** Those tell you if the expertise is landing.

### The first video

Recommendation for the channel-opening video:

> **"I spent 18 years inside the most data-rigorous company in the world. Here's what I learned about AI that no one is telling you."**

- Anchors the credential immediately (without naming the employer)
- Promises a perspective the viewer can't get elsewhere
- Sets the bar: this is going to be a channel that pays off
- Naturally introduces System // Self framing
