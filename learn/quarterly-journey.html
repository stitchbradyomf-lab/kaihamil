Key Insights

From Products to Method

Started building products (Valé, Groove Pal). Discovered the real asset is the method that produces them. Products become worked examples, not businesses.

Content vs. Building

Creating content feels like progress but often isn't. The hard work is specific, pride-worthy building — not documentation of intent.

Failure as Curriculum

The 7 AM cron job that never worked became the opening story of the course. Real failure is more valuable than manufactured success.

Marissa as Data Point

Couples Valé didn't land. Not a verdict — information. Either she's not the market, the thing wasn't good, or over-fitting occurred. All useful.

System // Self

The organizing principle: System = doing things better (efficiency). Self = doing better things (wisdom). Both required, different metrics.

The Path Forward

Education as near-term income. Workshop as lead generation. Community of independent doers as long-term value. Not four businesses — one practice.

Current Focus

Teaching the method through worked examples. Building a community of system-developers. Preparing for Phase 1 course launch.
