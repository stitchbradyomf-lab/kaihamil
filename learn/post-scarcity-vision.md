# Post-Scarcity Vision

*A world where needs are met, worth is redefined*

---

## The Premise

AI designs and builds everything.
No companies needed.
All production registered and tracked.
Malfeasance prevented, not punished.

---

## The Result

**Needs always met.**

No survival anxiety.
No economic coercion.
No "I have to work to eat."

---

## The New Economy

**What people do:**
- Leisure
- Gatherings
- Contribution

**Where worth comes from:**
- Medicine
- Social work
- Care
- Teaching
- Art

The fields that remain when material scarcity disappears.

---

## The Assumptions

- Nature still does some damage (storms, disease)
- But "much less than before"
- AI handles prediction, prevention, response

---

## The Unresolved

If AI builds everything, and AI prevents most harm:
- What do people actually *do*?
- Is contribution still needed?
- What gives life meaning?

The vision assumes abundance solves purpose. But purpose may be orthogonal to need.

---

## The Connection to Intension

In this world, the app becomes:
- Not tracking time for productivity
- Tracking attention for *meaning*
- The gap: "I have everything, what do I focus on?"

Boredom as luxury becomes boredom as *condition*.

---

## The Question

> What would you pursue if you didn't have to pursue anything?

The answer is the intension.
