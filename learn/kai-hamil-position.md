# Kai Hamil Position

*What I actually do*

---

## The Flip

**Old frame:** What can we automate?

**New frame:** What do we NOT want to automate?

---

## What I Am

Not an expert in automation.

An expert in **design with intention** — using technology for betterment.

---

## The Practice

Recognizing and promoting:
- Activities that matter
- Time spent with purpose
- Focus that is intentional

Avoiding effort that creates **drift from humanity**.

---

## The Core Question

> "What do you WANT your people to do?"

Not what can AI do. What should humans do?

---

## The Realization

AI cannot fully automate businesses. This is not failure. This is **clarity**.

The opportunity: redesign work so humans do what humans should do.

---

## The Demonstration

Groove Pal, Valé, daily brief — these are not "look what AI can do."

They are **"look what I chose to automate, and what I chose to keep human."**

- Automated: tracking, routing, logistics
- Human: listening, sharing, trust, judgment

---

## The Workshop

**"Design What Your People Do: A Workshop on Intentional Automation"**

The pitch:
- AI can do more than you think
- AI cannot do everything you hope
- The gap is where your value lives
- Let's design for that

---

## The Deeper Position

Not selling AI expertise.

Selling **clarity about human work**.

The systems are examples of intentional choices.

The job at Bloomberg is the same work at scale.

Kai Hamil is the practice of making these choices visible, teachable, repeatable.
