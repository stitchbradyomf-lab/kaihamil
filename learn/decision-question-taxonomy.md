# The Decision-Question Taxonomy

**Established:** June 21, 2026
**Composes with:** `learn/youtube-thesis.md`, `learn/youtube-first-10.md`

---

## The Core Insight

> Data is useless until you know what question you're trying to answer.

Information without a decision-question attached is just noise. The question type **determines what counts as relevant data** — and what should be filtered out.

This is the missing operational layer beneath the channel's koan:

> Data = new event streams + existing content.
> Outcome = decision-making capability.
>
> **But which decision?**

That third sentence is what most systems and most thinking miss.

---

## The Four Canonical Decision-Question Types

### 1. Diagnostic — *"Is anything failing or broken?"*

The question of operational health.

- Triggers: anomalies, exceptions, threshold violations
- Required data: real-time signals + a baseline to compare against
- Time-sensitivity: HIGH (urgency increases with delay)
- Decision output: stop / fix / escalate / monitor

**Examples in Kyle's life:**
- "Did the morning brief actually run?"
- "Is the PocketBase instance up?"
- "Did Hudson have a hard day at school?"
- "Did the vendor SLA get breached this week?"

**The data motif applied:** New event streams = critical. Existing content = the baseline that defines "broken."

### 2. Allocative — *"Where should I invest time/resources/attention?"*

The question of opportunity cost.

- Triggers: planning cycles, capacity changes, new openings
- Required data: comparative scoring across alternatives + capacity available
- Time-sensitivity: MEDIUM (urgency depends on opportunity decay)
- Decision output: prioritize / defer / decline / batch

**Examples in Kyle's life:**
- "Should I work on Life & Times or the Workshop tonight?"
- "Which of these client opportunities matters most?"
- "Where does the next dollar of marketing go?"
- "Is this family week worth saying no to work for?"

**The data motif applied:** Existing content = the inventory of options + their historical performance. New event streams = what's changing about the opportunity landscape.

### 3. Directional — *"Where is this trending, and what does it imply?"*

The question of trajectory and pattern.

- Triggers: review cycles, strategic checkpoints, gut-check moments
- Required data: time-series + comparative baselines + leading indicators
- Time-sensitivity: LOW (urgency is about pivot timing, not crisis)
- Decision output: continue / accelerate / pivot / wind down

**Examples in Kyle's life:**
- "Is Kai Hamil actually building momentum?"
- "Is my Time & Focus score trending up or down this month?"
- "Are the videos finding the right audience?"
- "Is the Bloomberg role moving toward something I'd want in 3 years?"

**The data motif applied:** Existing content = the trend. New event streams = the latest data points testing the trend.

### 4. Identity / Values — *"Is this aligned with who I'm trying to become?"*

The question of self-coherence.

- Triggers: friction moments, ethical decisions, big commitments
- Required data: personal principles + lived behavior comparisons + emotional signal
- Time-sensitivity: VARIABLE (sometimes immediate, sometimes long-considered)
- Decision output: yes / no / change myself / change my system

**Examples in Kyle's life:**
- "Does this opportunity move me toward 'creation aided by systems' or away from it?"
- "Am I being a good partner / father / son this week?"
- "Does this content actually represent what I believe?"
- "Am I trading presence for productivity in a way I'll regret?"

**The data motif applied:** Existing content = self-knowledge (USER.md, SOUL.md, North Star). New event streams = the situation you're being asked to commit to.

## Why This Matters For The Channel

The four question types are **a free way to structure any video.**

Most data/AI/productivity creators answer a tool question ("how do I X?"). Kyle's edge is answering a **decision question** ("what should I do, given X?").

For each video in the queue (`learn/youtube-first-10.md`), the script can be sharpened by asking: **which question type does this video help the viewer answer?**

| Video | Question Type | Sharpened framing |
|-------|---------------|-------------------|
| #1 (18 years) | Identity / Values | "Should you trust AI hype, given your career?" |
| #2 (Data 101) | All four | "What kind of question is your data trying to answer?" |
| #3 (The Center) | Identity / Values | "Are you choosing a position or being dragged into one?" |
| #4 (mornings) | Diagnostic + Allocative | "Is my day starting broken, and where should I aim it?" |
| #5 (three generations) | Directional | "Where is data work going?" |
| #6 (AI strategy questions) | Allocative + Identity | "What do you WANT your people to do?" |
| #7 (future of work) | Identity / Values | "Are you trying to do less of a dying job?" |
| #8 (relationship graph) | Diagnostic + Identity | "Who am I losing track of?" |
| #9 (Future of Vendor Ops) | Allocative | "Where should your vendor budget actually go?" |
| #10 (assembly manifesto) | Identity / Values | "What kind of worker are you choosing to be?" |

This is the layer that makes the videos *useful* instead of just informative. Every video should leave the viewer better able to answer a question they were already half-asking.

## Why This Matters For Stitch's Work

When Stitch is supporting Kyle's decisions, **always identify which question type is in play.** The data Stitch fetches, the framing Stitch offers, and the urgency Stitch attaches should match the question type:

- **Diagnostic** → fast, factual, anomaly-highlighted, action-oriented
- **Allocative** → comparative, scored, capacity-aware, opportunity-cost framed
- **Directional** → trend-visualized, baseline-aware, pivot-timing framed
- **Identity / Values** → principle-aware, mirror-back, slow-thinking encouraged

When Kyle asks an ambiguous question, **clarify by question type first.** Example:
- Kyle: *"How's Groove Pal going?"*
- Wrong answer: a metrics dump
- Right answer: *"Are you asking diagnostically (is anything broken?), allocatively (should you invest more time?), directionally (is it trending toward viability?), or identity-wise (does it still align with the North Star?)? Tell me the question and I'll get you the right answer."*

This is how Stitch becomes a decision partner rather than an information retrieval system.
