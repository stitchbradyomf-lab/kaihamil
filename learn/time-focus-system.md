# Time & Focus System

*The complete architecture*

---

## The Resource

**Time and focus** — tracked, finite, precious.

Not money. Not energy. Not productivity.

The resource is **attention deployed over time**.

---

## The Mechanisms

### 1. Tracking & Reporting
**The Time & Focus App**

- Captures expectations (what you intended)
- Captures reality (what you actually did)
- Reports variance (where drift occurred)

**Question it answers:** Where did my time and focus go?

---

### 2. Goal Setting
**The Human Involvement Framework**

- Asks: What do you want full control of?
- Asks: What do you want heavy involvement with?
- Asks: What do you want to approve?
- Asks: What do you want to be aware of?

**Question it answers:** What do I want my time and focus to serve?

---

### 3. Change Agent
**The Missing Mechanism**

Not direct change ("do less admin work").

**Second-order effect:** Fill your own cup to generate power for compounding systems.

**The logic:**
1. Invest time in systems (Level 2: Heavy Involvement)
2. Systems reduce administrative burden over time
3. Freed time goes to Level 1 (Full Control) activities
4. Level 1 activities fill the cup (meaning, energy, identity)
5. Full cup enables more system investment
6. Loop continues

**Question it answers:** How do I generate the power to change?

## The Key Insight

You cannot directly reduce administrative work.

You can:
1. **Track** where time goes (awareness)
2. **Clarify** what matters (intention)
3. **Invest** in systems that make admin unnecessary (compounding)
4. **Fill** your cup with meaning to sustain the investment (energy)

The change agent is not willpower. It is **the loop**.

---

## Application

### Personal
- Track time and focus daily
- Review weekly: where did I drift?
- Invest in one system per month
- Protect Level 1 activities ferociously

### Business
- Ask: What do we want our people doing?
- Track: Where do their time and focus go?
- Design: Systems that reduce low-level involvement
- Measure: Ratio of Level 1+2 to Level 3+4 over time

Not productivity. Not efficiency.

**Agency over attention.**
