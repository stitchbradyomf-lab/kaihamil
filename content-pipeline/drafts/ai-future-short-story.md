# AI Future Short Story

*A story about what comes after the barrier falls*

---

This story, like so many other stories just like it, starts in the same exact way; with words.

Words that are supposed to have meaning—our feeble attempt to transfer one human's perception of the world to another's. Turns out words only go so far...we must first recognize this limitation before proceeding on.

Do you recognize it? Say it out loud. "Words have limits!"

Do it. Say it out loud. Don't shout!

And so the author attempts to connect to an audience they cannot describe nor relate to—an audience whose perspectives are less visible than the depths of the East River. No context, easy manipulation, backwards incentives.

The goal of capitalism was to generate wealth and prosperity. It has. It just isn't distributed to benefit society as a whole. Why a human being in 2026 needs to spend 40 hours behind a desk just to prove their worth to society, while corporations like Apple make the iPhone 18(!?legal?!!?!) and we're supposed to be excited about that?

Let's be real, no one's excited about technology anymore. The inevitable future path isn't necessary. Or inevitable.

Because when technology is no longer the barrier, something else becomes the key constraint.

*(Ref: Theory of Constraints via The Goal—but make it of global society)*

So "what to build" becomes more important than "how to build it".

Product overtakes Technology.

Crowdsourced demand overtakes internal marketing. Ask for something and the market will deliver it. With AI firmly involved at every level of repetition beyond initial design and validation plan. Quality built in and guaranteed. Constant learning and improvement. Humans get out of the way of production and quality. Marketing, distribution, etc. All automated.

People gain their worth by creating social value. Spend 10 hours a week reading to the elderly? Spend 40 hours a week playing video games? Social worth will see it.

This future is possible; we have to want to buy it enough to make the market forces come together.
