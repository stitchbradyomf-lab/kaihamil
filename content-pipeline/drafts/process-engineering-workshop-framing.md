# Process Engineering Workshop Framing

*Designing workflows that serve purpose, not space*

---

## The Hook

Unloading the dishwasher. Carrying glasses from Miele dishwasher to soft-close cabinets. Realizing: **the kitchen design is wrong.**

We are acting *because of* the space, instead of having the space act *for us*.

This is the distinction. This is process engineering.

---

## What Process Engineering Is

Not woo-woo intentionality. Technical, rigorous, grounded.

**The questions:**
- What are your constraints?
- What can you control?
- Where will you see variability?

**The philosophy:**
Design according to purpose, not according to how it currently works.

---

## The Core Principle

A well-designed process emerges from the **best path from where you are to where you need to go**.

Synonymous with:
- Define your inputs
- Define your outputs
- The process engineering becomes feasible

Not simple. Feasible.

---

## Critical Constraints

Good process engineering depends on:

1. **What am I getting?** (inputs)
2. **What do I need to produce?** (outputs)

With surrounding context:
- Scientific validation
- Measurement, counting, metrics
- Health scores

**The goal:** Not perfect dashboards. A measurement system that defines **what is done and what is good**.

---

## Success Metrics

**How many things do you get right?**
**How many things do you get done?**

In that order.

Getting things done without getting them right:
- Partly done
- Negatively done (reducing value)
- Erroneous or incomplete output

---

## System Thinking

Process engineering applies to any system with:
- Series of inputs and outputs
- Governed not by central definition of good
- But by **relationship to other processes in the system**

---

## The Kitchen Example

Unloading dishes is not the point.

The point: **What should a kitchen provide?**

Think of it as a product:
- What value does it add?
- What function does it provide?
- What does it do for me that reduces my burden?

**Important caveat:** A kitchen is a distinctly human space. The act of cooking is not to be automated away.

---

## The Automation Question

In a world where you could automate anything, should you automate everything?

Have you really thought that through?

**Automation is a HOW.**  
**What's more important is the WHAT.**

Task. Product. Thing. Action. Uses of energy and resources.

---

## Workshop Applications

### Data Manufacturing Context
- Input: Raw data, requirements, constraints
- Output: Clean data, insights, decisions
- Process: The workflow between them
- Measurement: Quality scores, throughput, error rates

### Personal Context (Intension App)
- Input: Time, attention, capacity
- Output: Meaningful work, relationships, growth
- Process: How you deploy resources
- Measurement: Fulfillment, alignment, drift

### Business Context
- Input: Capital, labor, materials
- Output: Product, service, value
- Process: Operations, workflows, systems
- Measurement: Quality, efficiency, customer satisfaction

---

## The Deeper Frame

Process engineering is not about efficiency.

It's about **intentionality**—designing the path so the space works for you, not against you.
