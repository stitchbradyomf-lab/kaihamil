## Why This Matters

This exchange captures:

1. **The method in action** — real failure becomes teaching material
2. **Communication breakdown** — sarcasm vs. direct statement
3. **Iteration** — course improves through live feedback
4. **Authenticity** — no polish, no pretending systems work when they don't

The 7 AM cron job that never fired is now the opening story of the course. The instructor's frustration, the assistant's misreading, the recovery — it's all part of the curriculum.

## The Lesson

When you build systems, they fail. When you communicate about failure, clarity beats cleverness. When you teach, use your real failures — they're more valuable than manufactured success.

The daily brief that doesn't send at 7 AM is not a bug. It's the course.
