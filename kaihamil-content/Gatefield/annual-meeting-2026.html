Annual Meeting 2026
June 6, 2026

2026 Annual Meeting

Held at the East Island of Gatefield
Meeting Agenda

What we covered.

The following items were presented, discussed, and voted upon at the annual meeting.

1. Welcome & Introductions
President Kyle Brady called the meeting to order at 10:00 AM. Board members present: Kyle Brady (President), Thomas Convery (Vice President), James Weinberg (Treasurer), Denise Weinberg (Secretary). All members of the Landscape Committee were also present. In total, only 9 households were represented and so we agreed that any financial decisions would be made via proxy vote following the live meeting.
2. Treasurer's Report
Treasurer James Weinberg presented the 2025 financial summary and proposed 2026 budget. The association ended 2025 with a cash balance of $2,974.72. The 2026 budget projects $26,400 in revenue (dues from 22 households) and $22,900 in expenses, with a projected net positive cash flow of $3,500.
Budget Approved
3. Landscape Committee Report
Committee Lead Ann Mule, along with the Landscape Committee presented the spring and summer landscaping plan, including vinca planting at both entrances, spring cleanup and mulching, and completion of the three-year tree removal plan.
Projects Approved
4. Tree Removal - Final Phase
Proposal to remove 3 dead/almost dead trees (one large Linden and 2 purple leaf plum) on the island, including stump grinding. T&T Tree Services proposal of $2,075 was presented.
Approved
5. Spring Vinca Planting
Proposal to plant 25 flats of vinca flowers at both entrances prior to spring cleanup and mulching, including 2 free waterings. Cost: $1,125. Option for up to 4 additional waterings at $125 each.
Approved
6. Website & Communication Update
President Kyle Brady demonstrated the new Gatefield HOA website, including homeowner portal access, document repository, and annual meeting RSVP system. The site is now live at gatefield-hoa.netlify.app.
7. Open Discussion & Community News
Community announcements included congratulations to Maddie Carlton on her upcoming wedding (June 6, 2026), school end date (June 18, 2026), and general neighborhood updates.
Action Items

To-do follow-ups.

The following actions were assigned at the meeting with designated owners and timelines.

  • Fern Fried to coordinate with T&T Tree Services for tree removal scheduling. Target: June 2026
  • Fern Fried to coordinate spring vinca planting and schedule initial watering. Target: Late June 2026
  • Landscape Committee to coordinate spring cleanup and mulching following vinca planting. Target: June-July 2026
  • Kyle Brady to finalize website homeowner password distribution and update board contact information. Target: June 2026
  • James Weinberg to provide quarterly financial updates to the board and annual summary to homeowners. Ongoing
Votes Taken

All items requiring homeowner approval were presented and voted upon. Results below.

2026 Operating Budget

Approved Unanimously
Tree Removal - Final Phase
Approval of $2,075 expenditure for removal of 3 trees and stump grinding.
Approved Unanimously
Spring Vinca Planting
