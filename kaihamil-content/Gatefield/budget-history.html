Context

Understanding the variance.

Several factors explain the year-to-year fluctuations in our financial history.

2017-2018: Major tree removal and landscaping projects resulted in higher expenses. The association invested in long-term property improvements.

2019-2020: Dues decreased as some households faced financial hardship. Expenses remained steady, drawing down reserves.

2021: Continued recovery period with conservative spending and gradual dues normalization.

2022-2023: Return to full dues collection ($1,200/household). Stable operations with positive cash flow.

2024-2025: Increased investment in special projects including tree removal phases and entrance improvements. Higher expenses but necessary for property maintenance.

2026 (Projected): Return to positive cash flow with stabilized expenses and full revenue collection. The projected $10,070 ending balance provides healthy reserves for future needs.
