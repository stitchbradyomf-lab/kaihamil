Both versions are the same trip at heart: settle into Octant Ponta Delgada on the harbor, then move east to Octant Furnas in the thermal valley — two hotels, spa at each, and long stretches of downtime. The only thing that changes is one extra night in the city, which turns into a relaxed first day and a second unhurried excursion day. More nights here means more space around the few things we do, not a busier schedule.

The route · west to east

Sleep west, then east — and play all along the line. The two ringed markers are the hotels; the dotted arc is the one move between them. Everything else is an easy day out from whichever base you're at.
