São Miguel Six nights, west to east — decompress, adventure, then thermal calm.

18–25 AUG 2026 EWR ⇄ PDL 6 NIGHTS 2 TRAVELERS 2 HOTELS

The plan traces the island from its green western craters to the steaming eastern valley. Three nights on the north coast at Pedras do Mar keep the active days — Sete Cidades, the crater lake, the west coast — within easy reach. Then three nights east at Octant Furnas trade motion for thermal water, gardens, and nothing to do. The even split costs a little more than a Furnas-light version, but it earns you Nordeste and a true day of rest.

Day by day

DAY 01WED 19 AUG

Arrive & decompress

Land around 08:40 and collect the rental. Ease into Ponta Delgada's old town and marina with a long lunch, check in at Pedras do Mar near 3, then nap off the red-eye. Resort spa and a quiet first dinner — nothing strenuous.

DAY 02THU 20 AUG

West — Sete Cidades

Vista do Rei and the short ridge walk to Boca do Inferno, looking down on the twin blue-and-green crater lakes. In the afternoon, Ponta da Ferraria — a hot spring inside an ocean tidal pool — timed to low tide. Mosteiros on the west coast if the light holds.

DAY 03FRI 21 AUG

The crater lake

Lagoa do Fogo in the morning, then the thermal forest pools at Caldeira Velha — book the first timed slot. Wind down in Ribeira Grande. Last night at Pedras do Mar.

DAY 04SAT 22 AUG

Move east

An unhurried checkout, with a stop at Gorreana — Europe's oldest tea plantation — on the drive. Check in at Octant Furnas and settle into the geothermal pools and the ochre thermal pool at Terra Nostra Garden.

DAY 05SUN 23 AUG

Nordeste

The island's most dramatic coast — the Ponta do Sossego viewpoints, or the Salto do Prego waterfall hike from Faial da Terra. Back to Furnas for the evening pools.

DAY 06MON 24 AUG

Do nothing, properly

Cozido lunch cooked in the volcanic ground, the hot pools at Poça da Dona Beija, and the couples massage at the Octant spa. Nowhere else to be.

DAY 07TUE 25 AUG

Home

Early checkout and the ~50-minute drive to PDL for the late-morning flight back to Newark.

Good to know

Tides
Ponta da Ferraria only works around low tide — check the table and aim Thursday afternoon to it.
Terra Nostra
Wear a dark swimsuit; the iron-rich water stains light fabric.
Drive times
Airport ↔ Pedras do Mar ~25 min · Pedras ↔ Furnas ~1 h · Furnas ↔ airport ~50 min.
Season
August is warm, sunny, and busy — the driest, sunniest month, with the warmest sea. Reserve everything early.
