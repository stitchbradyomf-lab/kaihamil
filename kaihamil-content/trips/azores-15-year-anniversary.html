The trip traces the island from the green western craters to the steaming eastern valley. Three nights at Octant Ponta Delgada on the harbor keep the active days — Sete Cidades, the crater lake, the west coast — within easy reach, with a rooftop pool and spa to ease off the red-eye. Then three nights east at Octant Furnas trade motion for thermal water, gardens, and nothing to do. Only a couple of fixed anchors; everything else is optional.

The route · west to east

Sleep west, then east — and play all along the line. The two ringed markers are the hotels; the dotted arc is the single move between them.

Day by day

FRI 21 AUG

Arrive & decompress · city

Land ~08:40 and collect the rental. Ease into Ponta Delgada's old town and marina with a long lunch, check in at the Octant near 3, then nap off the red-eye. Rooftop pool, spa, and a quiet first dinner.

SAT 22 AUG

West — Sete Cidades ★

Vista do Rei and the short ridge walk to Boca do Inferno over the twin blue-and-green lakes, then a booked 13:00 one-hour kayak rental for two on the lake with Futurismo (booking 371519676). After that, Ponta da Ferraria — a hot spring inside an ocean pool — timed to low tide. Dinner as the reward: the farm tasting menu at Quinta dos Sabores (8-course, 19:30; ~25 min out, near Ribeira Grande) — arrive early to walk the farm.

SUN 23 AUG

The crater lake · gentle

An easy start — out around 8:45 for Lagoa do Fogo while the crater is still clear of cloud. Then the short drop down to Caldeira Velha, 10:30–12:00: warm pools under a forest waterfall, already booked with the locker sorted.

Afternoon — pick one: wander Ribeira Grande (old town, the eight-arched bridge, a long café lunch — passive and easy), or swap in Salto do Cabrito (a waterfall reached by gravel road and a steep metal staircase — active, if you've got the legs). One or the other, not both.

MON 24 AUG

Move east — chapel & a seaside lunch

Unhurried checkout, then the south road east. Lunch at Bar Caloura when it opens at noon — grilled catch-of-the-day at an ocean-side table, with a swim in the volcanic cove (no reservations; arrive early). Flag Marissa's octopus/squid allergy to staff — stick to whole grilled fish. Then climb the staircase to Ermida de Nossa Senhora da Paz above Vila Franca do Campo, and on to Furnas: check in and settle into the geothermal pools and Terra Nostra.

TUE 25 AUG

Open day · downtime

Nothing owed. Options if the mood strikes: Gorreana, Europe's oldest tea plantation, a short hop north; the dramatic Nordeste coast and the Ponta do Sossego viewpoints; or the Salto do Prego waterfall loop. Otherwise, just the hot springs and gardens.

WED 26 AUG

Thermal & massage ★

Cozido lunch cooked in the volcanic ground, the hot pools at Poça da Dona Beija, and the couples massage at the Octant spa. Nowhere to be.

THU 27 AUG

Home

Early checkout and the ~50-minute drive to PDL for the late-morning flight back to Newark.
