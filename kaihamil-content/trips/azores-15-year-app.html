Journey arc

How the week should feel

Start
Recover first, do not perform.Arrival day is for walking the marina, eating slowly, and letting the island come to you instead of trying to win the first day.
Middle
Spend the legs early.Sete Cidades and Lagoa do Fogo carry the dramatic movement while the energy is fresh, with real anchors already holding the pace in place.
Finish
Slide into Furnas.The east side is where the trip softens: whale watch, pools, Poça at dusk, massage, cozido, and the kind of ending that makes going home feel almost rude.

Tiny reminders

The details that save the day

  • Check Ferraria's low-tide windowThe west day is better when you time the ocean hot spring instead of just showing up hopeful.
  • Dark swimsuits really matterTerra Nostra and the thermal pools can stain lighter fabrics.
  • Wednesday lunch should be bookedCozido is better when it is inevitable, not opportunistic.
  • Set the first navigation stop before getting in the carIt lowers friction and prevents a sleepy airport start from turning messy.
  • The move-east day is the only day with real bunching riskDo less if the catamaran or lunch runs long. Furnas is not improved by arriving frazzled.
