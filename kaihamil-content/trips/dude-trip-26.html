The Days

72 hours, one summit

FRI 6Arrive + downtown day

UA 3546 lands 11:17am. Straight downtown for lunch, Falls Park, and the Liberty Bridge in daylight, then into the house when check-in opens at 4. Groceries delivered. Dinner back on Main Street or first night at the house - group's call.

SAT 7Mountain day

Pick one of the three hikes below - all ~45 minutes from the house, all at peak color. Back by dark for the hot tub, sauna, and poker.

Table Rock Summit ~7 MI · 2,000+ FT · STRENUOUS

The signature Upstate climb - a real workout through Carrick Creek Falls and Governor's Rock to a huge summit view. The one we'll talk about after.

Raven Cliff Falls 4.4 MI · ~1,100 FT · MODERATE

Overlook of a 420-ft waterfall - the tallest in South Carolina - off Caesars Head. Shorter day; add the drive-up Caesars Head overlook for sunset on the way out.

Raven Cliff + Dismal Loop 8.1 MI · 2,100 FT · HARD

The full loop - crosses a suspension bridge directly above the falls. 4.6 stars across 2,000+ AllTrails reviews. Steep in places; the biggest day of the three.

ATVs: guided UTV outfits are 1.5+ hrs away in the NC mountains - eats the day. Rafting season ends before November. The hike is the play; Paris Mountain (10 min) has easy trails for a Sunday leg-stretcher.

SUN 8Slow day + the big cook
