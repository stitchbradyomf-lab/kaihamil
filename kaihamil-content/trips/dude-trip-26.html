SUN 8Big hike + possible chef

Daytime anchor: pick one of the three hikes below - all about 45 minutes from the house and timed for peak fall color.

Table Rock Summit ~7 MI · 2,000+ FT · STRENUOUS

The signature Upstate climb - a real workout through Carrick Creek Falls and Governor's Rock to a huge summit view. The one we'll talk about after.

Raven Cliff Falls 4.4 MI · ~1,100 FT · MODERATE

Overlook of a 420-ft waterfall - the tallest in South Carolina - off Caesars Head. Shorter day; add the drive-up Caesars Head overlook for sunset on the way out.

Raven Cliff + Dismal Loop 8.1 MI · 2,100 FT · HARD

The full loop - crosses a suspension bridge directly above the falls. 4.6 stars across 2,000+ AllTrails reviews. Steep in places; the biggest day of the three.

Sunday dinner: possible in-home dinner with host-recommended chef Alison Beach. Availability still needs to be confirmed.

Open opportunity: someone can own the final hike selection and trail logistics. If the chef is unavailable, Sunday dinner is open too.
