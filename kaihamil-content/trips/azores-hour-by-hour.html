EFFORT

Active + "pain." The good kind of hard — hikes, climbs. Punctuation, not the main course.

ENGAGED

Active + pleasure. Lively enjoyment — thermal swims, town wanders, dinners out.

RESTFUL

Passive + pleasure. The indulgent core — soaks, the massage, slow meals, lounging.

LOGISTICS

The waste to minimize — driving, check-in/out, the car return, security.

SLEEP

The baseline you protect so everything else lands well.

Balance bars below each date show the mix of waking hours (sleep excluded). They're approximate — the point is the shape, not the decimals.

Read it as a shape: restful-dominant (~half the waking hours), which is right for an anniversary trip — the indulgence is the point, not a reward you have to earn. Effort sits at ~12%, concentrated in two or three clean beats (the ridge on Saturday, the optional waterfall Tuesday) so it punctuates the week instead of grinding through it. Logistics holds near ~14% and most of it lands on two days — the one hotel move and the flight home — leaving the middle days almost waste-free. And the arc matters as much as the totals: you open soft (recovery), spike effort early while you're freshest, then slope down into pure pleasure, closing on the massage and the cozido. Hard when you have the legs for it, easy when you've earned it.
