# Fieldhouse

**A membership space built for professional parents — where the work gets done and the kids don't want to leave.**

---

## Concept

Fieldhouse is a membership-based third place for professional parents and their children — combining co-working ergonomics, café quality, ambient child supervision, and physical play at a scale kids find legendary.

Not a trampoline park. Not a children's museum. Not a library. Not a gym. **The best parts of each, without the worst of any.**

---

## The Core Insight

Every parent has sat next to the birthday cake, phone in hand, wishing they could open their laptop — but knowing they couldn't. Fieldhouse is the place where they can.

---

## Location

Target market: Bucks County, Pennsylvania
- Newtown / Yardley / Langhorne corridor
- Median HHI $131K-$166K
- 10K-18K SF flex/industrial conversion

---

## Membership Tiers

- **Solo:** $129/month (one adult)
- **Family:** $229/month (two adults + up to three children)
- **Founding:** $179/month (locked-in rate, first 50 members)

---

## Key Rules

1. Laptops are welcome
2. No birthday parties. Ever.
3. Kids are supervised — not managed
4. The coffee is real
5. No screens in the play zones
6. Membership is the product
