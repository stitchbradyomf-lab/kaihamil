# Fieldhouse — One Page

**A membership space built for professional parents — where the work gets done and the kids don't want to leave.**

---

## The Insight

Every parent has sat next to the birthday cake, phone in hand, wishing they could open their laptop — but knowing they couldn't.

**Fieldhouse is the place where they can.**

The post-2020 remote-work shift created a permanent category of professional parents who need to work, want to be near their kids on weekends, and have no good third place for both. Coffee shops are hostile to kids. Kids' play places are hostile to adults. Country clubs are designed for a different era.

---

## The Product

A single ~12,000-15,000 SF premium membership space with three integrated zones:

- **The Fieldwork** — Adult coworking café (real coffee program, sound-managed work zones, sightlines to play)
- **The Yard** — Legendary kids' play (signature climbing structure, age-zoned, ambient supervision)
- **The Workshop** — Maker space for parent-kid overlap (light fabrication, drawing, design)

**Membership-only. No birthday parties. No day passes. All-inclusive pricing.**

| Tier | Price |
|------|-------|
| Solo | $129/mo |
| Family | $229/mo |

## The Real Estate

**Target:** 10,000 - 18,000 SF flex/industrial, 16+ ft ceilings, strong parking ratio.

**Corridor:** Newtown / Yardley / Langhorne (Bucks County PA, median HHI $131K-$166K).

**Lease comp:** $13-$25/SF NNN. Indoor model decouples from retail visibility. Long-term tenant, landlord-friendly.

---

## The Moat

**No competitor threads:** premium adult experience + serious kid play + coworking + premium F&B + membership + no birthday parties.

The "no birthday parties ever" rule sacrifices $200K-$400K/year of easy revenue per location. **That's the moat.** Competitors won't replicate the sacrifice. The members who want this will pay for it precisely because of it.
