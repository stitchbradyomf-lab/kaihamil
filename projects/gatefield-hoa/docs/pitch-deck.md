# Gatefield HOA Website
## Presentation for Annual Meeting — June 6, 2026

---

## Slide 1: The Problem

**Last year, we agreed to digitize our documents.**

- Photocopying costs for bylaws, CC&Rs, meeting packets
- Residents asking for copies, no central place to find them
- Board members managing documents via email and text
- No easy way to share meeting schedules or news

**The question:** How do we make HOA information accessible without adding cost or complexity?

---

## Slide 2: The Solution

**A simple website for Gatefield HOA**

What it does:
- **Documents:** Bylaws, CC&Rs, forms — all in one place
- **Meetings:** Schedule, agendas, minutes — always current
- **News:** Updates without printing newsletters
- **Contact:** Direct email to board members

What it costs:
- **$0** to build (I did it)
- **$1/month** to host
- **$0** for updates (board does it)

---

## Slide 3: How It Works

**For Residents:**
- Visit the website
- Find what you need
- Download documents
- See meeting info

**For the Board:**
- Edit content via GitHub (simple)

- Changes go live in 2-3 minutes
