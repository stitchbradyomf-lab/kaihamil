## Board Responsibilities

- **President:** Presides over meetings, represents HOA to community
- **Vice President:** Assists president, presides when president unavailable
- **Treasurer:** Manages finances, prepares budgets and financial reports
- **Secretary:** Records minutes, maintains official records
- **Member at Large:** General board duties, committee oversight

## Elections

Board elections are held annually at the June meeting. Terms are two years.

Interested in serving? [Download the nomination form](/assets/documents/form-board-nomination.pdf) or contact any board member.
